All four together · Electromagnetic waves

A changing E makes B, a changing B makes E, and the pair travels as light

In empty space there is no charge and no current, so only the two "changing field" terms survive. Law 3 and Law 4 feed each other, and the fields carry themselves forward at one fixed speed, set entirely by μ0 and ε0.

Two constants measured with magnets and capacitors on a lab bench give the speed of light. Maxwell did this in 1862 and concluded that light is an electromagnetic wave.

The loop that sustains itself

Where E is changing fastest, Law 4 says a B field must curl around it. That B is itself changing, so Law 3 says an E field curls around it. Each field regenerates the other one step further along. Laws 1 and 2 add that in empty space neither field has sources, so the wave is purely transverse.

One wave, every band

Radio, microwaves, infrared, visible light, ultraviolet, X‑rays and gamma rays are the same wave at different frequencies. All travel at 299,792,458 m/s in vacuum. Drag the slider across sixteen orders of magnitude: only the wavelength and the energy per photon change.

Reading the picture

E and B rise and fall together, at right angles to each other and to the direction of travel. In real units B is smaller by a factor of c, so it is scaled up here to be visible. The wave moves in the direction of E × B.
