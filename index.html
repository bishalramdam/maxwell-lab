Four equations. Five lab benches.

James Clerk Maxwell summed up all of electricity, magnetism and light in four equations. Each bench below lets you run the experiment behind one of them: move charges, cut magnets, push magnets through coils and watch compasses swing.

The four laws

Electric field spreads out from charge. The flux through any closed surface equals the charge inside.

∇ · B = 0 ∯ B · dA = 0

There are no magnetic charges. Every magnetic field line closes on itself, so net flux out of a closed surface is zero.

Reading the symbols

The differential form describes what happens at a single point. The integral form describes a whole surface or loop, which is what you measure on each bench. They say the same thing.

∇ ·Divergence: how much a field spreads out from a point (a source or sink). ∇ ×Curl: how much a field swirls around a point. ∯Integral over a closed surface: total flux out. ∮Integral around a closed loop: total circulation.
